HOW TO PLAY
  1. Follow the arrows painted on the road — they route you street by street. The floating arrow over your roof points at the next turn.
  2. Pull up at the cyan beam and slow down to let your passenger in.
  3. Race to the gold beam before the meter runs out. Arrive early and the tip goes up.
  4. Hold Space through a corner to drift. The longer you hold the slide, the bigger the multiplier — and it all converts to cash.
  5. Crash and you lose the drift you were building. Land it first.
  6. Everything you earn banks into the garage fund. Spend it on a quicker limo with more grip.
